Beginner Guide

🌱 What to do in your first week

Heartopia rewards consistency more than grinding. The fastest way to fall behind is to skip days; the fastest way to get ahead is a short daily routine. Here's the order that works best for new players:

  1. Head to the Codes page first for free starting rewards — dcthx4u expires June 29
  2. Complete the tutorial main quest to unlock the D.G. Guild system — almost every feature in the game is gated behind Guild level
  3. Complete 5 Resident Requests daily (+50 Medals). They do not stack, so missed days are gone for good
  4. Find Andrew to unlock riding and Vanya to unlock fishing — both are worth prioritizing in week one
  5. Caught fish can be gifted to Vanya for friendship points, or sold to Albert Jr. for Coins if you need cash instead
  6. On rainy or rainbow days, look for the hidden NPC Doris — she sells rare recipes that aren't available anywhere else
💡 Use the Reset Timer to set a reminder — missing the 6:00 AM daily reset means losing Resident Request medals, which are the most reliable source of Guild progress.
