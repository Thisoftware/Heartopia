🌳 Oak-Oak (Oak Spirit)

Oak-Oak is not a regular pet butan extremely rare hidden creature that wanders random locations across town. It may take days or even weeks to encounter one.
  1. Oak-Oak appears in a different random location each day — no fixed route
  2. When you spot it, stop moving and approach slowly — it will flee if startled
  3. The community is still tracking Oak-Oak sightings. Known areas: Amazing Forest and near Onsen Cliff
  4. Interacting with Oak-Oak grantsa mystery reward(random each time)

✨ Screenshot your Oak-Oak encounter and share it with the community — it's a rare moment many players dream of!
