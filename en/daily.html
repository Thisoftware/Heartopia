Energy Management

⚡ Energy System

Energy bar (fully restored after daily reset)

🪓 Chopping · 🎣 Fishing · ⛏️ Mining use Energy; walking · talking · building do NOT

  1. Daily 6:00 AM Energy fully restores at server reset — schedule energy-intensive activities right after
  2. When Energy drops below 20 you can only walk — use your bike to stay efficient
  3. Eat food to quickly restore Energy — Mixed Jam restores 22 points; advanced dishes like Truffle Mushroom Pie restore much more
  4. Sleeping is the easiest passive recovery — put your character to bed before logging off
  5. When Energy is depleted you can still doEnergy-free activities: arrange furniture, chat with NPCs, or browse shops
Weekly Tasks

📅 Weekly Goals

  1. Weekly tasks offer far bigger rewards than daily tasks — prioritize them over side activities
  2. After D.G. Level 13, weekly medal rewards jump to 30–50 — a key acceleration phase
  3. Resets Saturday 6:00 AM — confirm your weekly task completion before logging off Friday
  4. You must manually click "Claim" — rewards won't auto-deliver to your inventory
