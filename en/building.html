🏗️ Building Guide

Plot expansion, materials, and practical decorating tips

Your starting plot is small, and it's tempting to cram furniture into it right away. A better approach is to think about layout first — decide where rooms will go, expand when you can afford it, and only then start decorating. Nothing in Heartopia's building system is permanent, so experimenting costs nothing but a little time.

🏗️ Home building is Heartopia's core gameplay. You can always start over — no mistakes are permanent! Tip: lock in the frame first, then place furniture.

How to Expand

Raise D.G. Level to Unlock

Co-op Building Unlock

D.G. Level 8

  1. Start with 3 plots. Raise your D.G. Level to gradually unlock more. Each expansion costs Coins and specific Materials
  2. Find Bob (Architect) to confirm current expansion costs — required materials vary by level
  3. Plan your overall layout before expanding. You can demolish existing walls to open up space afterward
  4. D.G. Level 8 unlocks Co-op Building, allowing you to invite other players to decorate together
  5. D.G. Level 27 unlocks Interactive Furniture Upgrades, granting access to exclusive furniture sets not sold in regular shops

Building Tips

💡 Practical Building Tips

  1. Use the top-down view (Top View) first for furniture placement — much clearer than third-person perspective
  2. Lock in the frame first (walls, stairs, floors) before placing furniture — adjusting structure is easier than rearranging furniture
  3. Place stairs and add flooring above to create a second floor; stack multiple times for lofts or rooftop terraces
  4. The exterior walls and yard matter just as much as the interior — don't neglect them
  5. After finishing your build, save it as a blueprint — revert any time, or share it with the community
  6. Unused furniture should not be deleted — store it for later. It may complete a set for bonus rewards
  7. Prize Bubbles reset daily and spawn randomly at Town Square, Onsen Cliff, Fishing Village, Amazing Forest, and more — patrol daily to collect them
🎨 Any furniture can be dyed and personalized — craft a pattern at the workbench and apply it. Layering rugs, placing small items on tables, and hanging wall decor all add depth.
