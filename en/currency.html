💎 Currency Guide

All 6 currencies, what they're for, and how to get them for free

Heartopia layers several currencies on top of each other, and it's easy to spend Heart Diamonds on something you could have unlocked for free with a bit of patience. Knowing which currency feeds into which before you buy anything saves a surprising amount of money over time.

⚠️ There are many currencies in this game. New players should first understand which ones are free before deciding to spend.

💎 Currency Overview

💎 Heart Diamonds Paid

Top-tier universal currency — purchase almost all premium content

Primary source: real-money purchase

🌙 Moonlight Crystals Free/Paid

Used to purchase Pink Gacha Coins

Daily tasks, events, Heart Diamond exchange

🎫 Pink Gacha CoinsFree/Paid

Used to pull special items and skins

Moonlight Crystal exchange / daily tasks

🎟️ Exhibition Pass Free/Paid

Open limited event loot boxes for exclusive furniture and outfits

Event rewards / Heart Diamond purchase

🏅 Contribution Medals Free

Raise D.G. Guild level to unlock game features

Daily tasks, NPC requests

⭐ Wishing Stars Free/Paid

Pull from limited banners for outfits and furniture

Events, login rewards, Heart Diamond exchange

🆓 Free-to-Play Currency Tips

  1. Use Contribution Medals to raise Guild level first — it unlocks more free content
  2. Accumulate Moonlight Crystals from daily tasks and focus on one banner at a time
  3. Exhibition Passes can be obtained free during events — check event schedules in advance
  4. Limited content usually returns in future events — don't feel pressured to spend if you miss out
  5. Prioritize items that help game progression over purely cosmetic ones
