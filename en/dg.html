🎖️ The D.G. (Developer's Guild) level is the main progression bar. Almost every core feature requires it. Free and paid players earn the same daily medals — consistency beats spending.
How to Level Up

🏅 How to Earn Contribution Medals

Daily Requests ★★★

Complete 5 Resident Requests per day, each gives +10 Medals, total +50 Medals per day

Resets at 6:00 AM server time daily — they don't stack! Most reliable source

Main Quest ★★★

Advance main quest tasks — each milestone gives plenty of Medals and unlocks new features

About 90% of content requires main quest progress to unlock — don't just do daily tasks

Weekly Tasks ★★☆

Weekly Goals，Reward 30–50 Medal（After D.G. Level 13unlock more）

Resets Saturday 6:00 AM — plan your weekly goals in advance

Mini-game Events ★☆☆

Event Ranking Rewards, extra +5–15 Medals

Notable gains after D.G. Level 13 — worth joining

Tips & Steps

📋 Optimal D.G. Leveling Route

  1. Open the phone icon → find the "D.G. Member Guild" icon (star shape, top-left) to check your current Medals and upgrade cost
  2. Every day,complete 5 daily Requests first, then advance the main quest, then do other activities
  3. Don't forget to complete yourweekly goals before Saturday — the rewards far exceed daily tasks
  4. Once you have enough Medals, manually tap "Level Up" — it won't upgrade automatically
  5. After leveling up, immediately check new unlocks and try them — some have time limits
