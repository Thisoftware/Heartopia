💡 Bug Catching Efficiency Tips

  1. Night in the Amazing Forest yields the most Rare Insects. Legendary insects have the highest spawn rate during Rainbow weather
  2. Walk slowly toward insects — running scares them away. Swing your net from directly above for the highest hit rate
  3. Gifting Rare Insects to Naniwa gives far more Friendship than Common bugs — save your rare catches for her
  4. Selling Common Insects to Albert Jr. is more profitable than gifting them — clear your inventory after each daily Bug Catching session
  5. Completing all 76 insects in your collection earns a Special title and rich rewards — work through one area at a time for maximum efficiency
