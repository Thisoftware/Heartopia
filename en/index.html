BeginnerBeginner Guide

🌱 What to do in your first week

  1. First, head to theCodes pagefor free rewards.dcthx4u expires June 29
  2. Complete the tutorial main quest to unlock the D.G. Guild system — almost every feature requires Guild level
  3. Complete 5 Resident Requests daily (+50 Medals). They do NOT stack — missed ones are gone forever
  4. Find Andrew to unlock riding, find Vanya to unlock fishing — do these two things first
  5. Caught fish can be gifted to Vanya for friendship, or sold to Albert Jr. for Coins
  6. On rainy/rainbow days, find hidden NPC Doris — she sells rare recipes unavailable elsewhere
💡 Use theReset Timerto set reminders — never miss the 6:00 AM reset! Daily requests are the most reliable source of Guild medals.
